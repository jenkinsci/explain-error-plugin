Also reads the workspace files that the failure log names, such as the source files of stack frames, compiler errors, failed tests or tracebacks. The log is read on the controller; no extra AI call is made.

Where the log names a line, the 40 lines before it and the 20 after it are sent, numbered, instead of the beginning of the file. At most 8 files are read, with the same safety rules as the configured workspace paths: absolute paths, path traversal, directories, symbolic links, secret files and build output directories are refused. These files have a budget of their own, the size of workspaceContextMaxBytes.

Requires Include Workspace Context. Secrets in the files are masked like every other piece of context, and the files are also sent with an auto-fix request.
